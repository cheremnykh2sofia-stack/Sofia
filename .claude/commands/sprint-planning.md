Sprint planning with capacity planning and risk assessment.

**Includes:**
1. Pre-planning data collection (backlog, velocity)
2. Sprint goal definition
3. Backlog grooming (P0/P1/P2/P3 priorities)
4. Task breakdown with estimates
5. Capacity planning per developer
6. Risk identification
7. Schedule with milestones
8. Success metrics
9. Action items

**Output:**
- Complete sprint plan document
- Linear tasks (if MCP configured)
- Team capacity allocation
- Risk mitigation strategies

**Example:**
```
/sprint-planning Sprint 15
/sprint-planning Q1-Week3
```
